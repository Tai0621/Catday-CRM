Every part below is lifted off the maker’s elevation: cream carcass, slatted vent capsule, glass door on centre hinges, the grey arch, the pale-green litter tray, the porthole in its grey panel. The cabinet never changes. Only the glass takes the state colour — which is how the wall reads in life, since you look through the door to see who is home.
